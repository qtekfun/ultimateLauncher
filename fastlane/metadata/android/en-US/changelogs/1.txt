First public release (0.1.0).
• Launcher based on AOSP Launcher3: no GMS, no telemetry and no Internet permission.
• Grid, icons, dock, drawer and folders tuned to measurements; tablet dock.
• Folders enlargeable to 2x2, backup and restore, import from other launchers.
• Bundled wallpapers, icon packs, swipe gestures and hidden apps with biometrics or PIN.
• Digital clock widget.
